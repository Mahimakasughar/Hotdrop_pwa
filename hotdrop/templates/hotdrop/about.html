ABOUT HOTDROP KITCHEN

HotDrop Cloud Kitchen is an innovative Web-based Cloud Kitchen Management System designed to simplify food ordering and kitchen operations. The platform allows customers to browse food items, add them to cart, place orders, and track them efficiently.

At the same time, admins can manage orders, view customer details, and update order status through an easy-to-use dashboard.

Our goal is to provide a smooth online food ordering experience with efficient backend management — making it ideal for restaurants, cloud kitchens, and food delivery start-ups.
